How to point a subdomain to your VPS

Updated on November 24, 2026, by the Jitterx Panel Cloud team

A subdomain is an easy-to-remember address, like my-server followed by our domain, that points to your server's public IP. On Jitterx Panel Cloud you can create one for free in seconds and use it, for example, as your CDN origin.

What an A record is

The DNS system turns names into IP addresses. An A record links a name to an IPv4 address. When someone types your subdomain, DNS answers with your server's IP and the connection goes there. Our subdomains use DNS only mode: they don't go through any proxy and point straight to your IP.

Step by step

  1. Go to the site and open the Domain section.
  2. Type the name you want: 2 to 32 characters, using lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
  3. Type your server's public IPv4. The preview shows how it will look: name.domain → IP.
  4. Tap Create A record. The subdomain resolves in under a minute.

Conditions

  • It's free and lasts 7 days; after that it's deleted automatically.
  • You can have 1 active record at a time.
  • Only public IPv4 addresses are accepted.

Using it as your CDN origin

When you get your CDN you can type this subdomain in the Origin field. Keep in mind it lasts 7 days: if it expires, the CDN won't find your server anymore. For ongoing use, it's more practical to use the public IP directly or a permanent domain of your own. If you change the origin later, do it with Change origin, free of charge.

How to check it works

From a computer you can run nslookup name.domain or ping name.domain: the answer should show your server's IP. You can also use any online DNS checker.

Responsible use. Subdomains are for your own servers. Using them for phishing, impersonation or illegal activity is not allowed.
