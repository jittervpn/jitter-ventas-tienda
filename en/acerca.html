About Jitterx Panel Cloud

Jitterx Panel Cloud is an online dashboard to create your own CloudFront CDN endpoint to your server and subdomains that point to your VPS. It's built for people who run their own server and want an easy, encrypted way to connect to it from apps like HTTP Custom or NetMod.

What we offer

  • CloudFront CDN to your server's domain or IP, with a host, your own path and a payload ready to copy. It costs $17.000 for 30 days and renews without changing any details.
  • Pay by card, Mercado Pago or PayPal: the service is created automatically once the payment is approved.
  • Free 7-day subdomains pointing to your VPS IP.
  • Guides to set up and understand each tool: see them all.

How we look after your account and data

You log in with a one-time code sent to your email, so there are no passwords to remember or leak. We keep only what the service needs to work and we don't sell your data. The details are in the privacy policy and the terms of use.

Responsible use

Our tools are for connecting to your own servers and protecting your privacy. We don't allow them to be used for illegal or abusive activity such as spam, attacks on third parties or fraud, and we reserve the right to suspend services that break this rule.
