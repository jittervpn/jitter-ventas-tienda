Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

At Jitterx Panel Cloud (jitterxcloud.shop) we respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for and what choices you have. By using the site you accept what is described here. This English version is a translation; if there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Jitterx Panel Cloud is a service that offers a CloudFront CDN to your server (paid) and temporary subdomains. For any question about your data, message us on our Telegram channel: https://t.me/jitterxpanelcloud.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: to log in or create your account we ask for your email address and send you a 6-digit verification code. We store your email and a user identifier; we don't use passwords. Codes are stored encrypted (hashed) and expire after 10 minutes.
  • CDN service data: the origin (your server's domain or IP) and the path you choose, the creation and expiry dates and, when the provider reports it, the service's traffic. This data is registered with the CDN provider so the service can work.
  • Browser identifier: a random code stored in your browser's local storage that we use for security purposes.
  • Subdomains: the subdomain name and the IP address you give us when you create it.
  • Payments: payments are processed by Mercado Pago or PayPal. We never see or store your card number. We only receive the payment identifier, its status, the amount and your email.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic browser details, which we use for security, to limit the number of requests and to prevent abuse.

3. What we use your data for

  • Creating and managing your account, your CDN services and your subdomains.
  • Delivering purchases and confirming payments.
  • Protecting the service against spam, scans and other abuse.
  • Answering your questions and improving the service.

We don't sell your personal data.

4. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's advertising cookie lets Google and its partners show you ads based on your browsing on this and other sites.
  • You can turn off personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can also see how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For more information about interest-based advertising, visit aboutads.info.

We also use your browser's local storage to keep your device identifier, your session and your language. You can clear it from your browser settings, although that may log you out.

5. Third-party services

To operate we use these providers, which may process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (AdSense).
  • Third-party ad networks (for example bauval.org), which may use cookies or browser identifiers to show ads in our guides. They are not loaded when you're logged in as a customer.
  • Mercado Pago and PayPal, to process payments.
  • Amazon CloudFront and the CDN API provider, to create and maintain your CDN services.
  • Cloudflare, to manage the subdomains' DNS records.
  • Vercel, to host the site and store the data the service needs.
  • Google (Gmail), to send login codes.
  • Telegram, if you choose to follow our channel or message us.

6. Data retention

We keep your data while you have an account or for as long as it's needed to provide the service. Subdomains are deleted when they expire. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

7. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access through one-time codes sent to your email. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data by messaging us on Telegram. If you live in Argentina, Law 25.326 on Personal Data Protection grants you these rights, and the Agency for Access to Public Information is the authority in charge of handling complaints.

9. Minors

The service is not intended for people under 18. If you believe a minor has given us data, contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the current version on this page with its update date.
